# Control Center Workflow Notes

See the [application README](../control_center/README.md) for setup and operational
details. The IC week runs Wednesday through Tuesday.

## Prepare Data

Read all configured newsletter deal sections, enumerate every financing,
deduplicate overlapping coverage, research missing facts and actual use cases,
normalize fields, and freeze the prepared evidence and dated exchange rates.
Missing newsletter coverage and genuinely unavailable fields remain visible.

## Review And Publish

Review every active financing against one frozen mandate. Route uncertain,
high-risk, and corrected records for independent evidence-backed verification.
Resolve every record to final Yes or No; Maybe is only an internal routing signal.
Include supported borderline fits as Yes, not unresolved placeholders.

Validate selected rows and publish the same final set to the Sheet, four-week
Stats, and weekly roundup. Confirm output agreement through readback. Linked
chart images in Docs still require a separate manual refresh.

## Feedback And Limits

Save human corrections and retrieve similar corrections for later reviews.
Corrections do not silently republish outputs; trigger a new review.
Diagnostic historical labels are not a blind representative accuracy benchmark.
The two-minute target excludes preparation and manual chart refresh.
